# Game Pitch

## Working title

**Nick's Luminous Valley**

## Premise

A cozy science-fantasy RPG about repairing a neglected village by observing problems, experimenting, inventing practical solutions, and watching those solutions reshape the community.

## Player fantasy

You are not merely collecting resources or optimizing a farm. You become the person who understands how the valley works well enough to improve it—and whose practical discoveries gradually uncover a larger mystery left by Nick's grandfather.

## Core loop

`Observe problem → gather evidence/materials → experiment → build → deploy → see village change`

## Tone

Warm, curious, hopeful, slightly mysterious. Scientific thinking should feel adventurous rather than clinical; village improvement should feel personal rather than abstract.

## Differentiating promise

The game should connect **understanding** to **visible consequence**. Inventions are not merely recipes or stat upgrades: they should alter places, routines, resources, relationships, and eventually the larger systems of the valley.

## First proof

**Nick's First Spark: The Broken Water Pump** tests the smallest complete version of that promise:
- a named community problem
- people who care about the outcome
- evidence/material gathering
- a constrained lab step
- deployment
- visible environmental recovery
- village-trust response
- a narrative mystery hook

The proof remains intentionally smaller than the eventual farming, social, JRPG, and infrastructure layers.
