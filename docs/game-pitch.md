# Game Pitch

## Working title
**Nick's Luminous Valley**

## Premise
A cozy science-fantasy RPG about repairing a neglected village by observing problems, experimenting, inventing practical solutions, and watching those solutions reshape the community.

## Player fantasy
You are not merely collecting resources or optimizing a farm. You are becoming the person who understands how the valley works well enough to improve it.

## Core loop
Observe problem → gather evidence/materials → experiment → build → deploy → see village change.

## Tone
Warm, curious, hopeful, slightly mysterious. Scientific thinking should feel adventurous rather than clinical; village improvement should feel personal rather than abstract.

## First proof
The Broken Water Pump vertical slice proves whether this loop is satisfying before larger farming, social, JRPG, or infrastructure systems are added.
