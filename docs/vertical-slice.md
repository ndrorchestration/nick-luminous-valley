# Vertical Slice — Nick's First Spark: The Broken Water Pump

## Purpose
Prove the smallest complete loop: observe a village problem → gather evidence/materials → experiment → build → deploy → observe a persistent world change.

## Player sequence
1. Meet Mira, the mechanic.
2. Meet Sora, the ecologist.
3. Recover copper wire, a cracked solar cell, a pipe fitting, and resin.
4. Use the lab to stabilize and assemble the repair.
5. Install the solar-assisted pump in the village garden.
6. Observe the garden revive and village trust respond.
7. Receive a strange signal from the grandfather's transmission tower.

## Acceptance criteria
- The project opens in Godot 4 and runs from the configured main scene.
- The player can move and interact without external assets.
- All four components can be collected.
- The lab refuses progression until all components are present.
- Installing the pump changes visible world state.
- The transmission-tower interaction provides a narrative end-state.
- Save/load reproduces player position, quest stage, collected parts, and pump state.

## Explicitly deferred
Full seasons, romance, party combat, procedural generation, open world, deep electricity simulation, full NPC schedules, large crafting trees, online features, voice acting, and multiplayer.
